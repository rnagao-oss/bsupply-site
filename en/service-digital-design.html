Digital: Design & Implementation

If management data isn't visible, investment decisions lose their speed.

B supply helps make management data visible and turns it into something usable for day-to-day decision-making. From designing KPI dashboards and implementing management accounting foundations to digitalizing existing operations and introducing tools that take root on the ground, we stay engaged through implementation rather than stopping at the concept stage. As principals who run our own businesses, we are exacting about whether a system is actually used on the ground.
