SERVICEOPERATION

Operations

Management decisions only become faster once the numbers are in order.

The more a business expands, the more the speed of grasping management data and making decisions can become a constraint on growth. B supply supports the "foundation" of management at the execution level, from building a management accounting foundation and KPI design to serving as an outsourced corporate planning function and improving day-to-day operations. As principals who run our own businesses, we build systems that actually run in operation, not just systems designed on paper.

01SOLUTIONS ── Solutions

Building Management Systems

We build a management accounting foundation that makes business figures visible, establishing a state where data-driven decision-making is possible.

KPI Design

We design KPIs grounded in business reality and translate them into a dashboard usable for management decisions.

Outsourced Corporate Planning

We serve as the outsourced corporate planning function itself, taking responsibility for formulating investment discipline and drafting mid-term plans.

Operational Improvement

We improve business operations themselves through rebuilding the management foundation, including cash flow and governance.

02APPROACH ── What Sets Our Support Apart

Staying Engaged Through Execution

Rather than stopping at system design, we stay engaged through execution, including dashboard construction and operational embedding.

The Perspective of a Management Principal

From our standpoint as principals who run our own businesses, we design management systems that actually function.

Connecting Strategy and Management Accounting

We design KPIs and management accounting together with management decisions such as investment decisions and mid-term business plans.

Taking On the Corporate Planning Function Itself

Beyond building the structure, we take on the role of corporate planning itself, supporting its continuous operation.
