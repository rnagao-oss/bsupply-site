SERVICEMARKETING & SALES

Marketing & Sales

A system that sells is born from not separating strategy and execution.

The more independently sales and marketing initiatives operate, the more diluted the results become. We design sales strategy and marketing strategy together, aligning everything from lead generation and pricing to external partnerships with the business's growth stage. Beyond strategy formulation, we embed ourselves in the execution of ad operations and sales activity, ensuring the strategy does not remain merely theoretical. We select initiatives with high return on investment, weighing both acquisition efficiency and close rate.

01SOLUTIONS ── Solutions

Sales Strategy Formulation & Execution

We design the process from generating sales meetings through to closing, and, where needed, take on the execution of sales activity itself.

Lead Strategy

We review lead acquisition channels and quality, and streamline sourcing and research systems.

Marketing Strategy & Operations

We redesign acquisition channels and messaging, taking end-to-end responsibility through ad operations.

Pricing

Based on market research and competitive analysis, we design pricing backed by solid rationale.

Alliances

For customer segments a company cannot reach on its own, we build new acquisition channels through partnerships with different industries.

02APPROACH ── What Sets Our Support Apart

Integrated Engagement Across Strategy and Execution

Rather than stopping at drawing up the strategy, we embed ourselves in the practical work of ad operations and alliance building. We proceed while confirming whether the initiatives we formulate actually function on the ground.

A Commitment to Data-Driven Decision-Making

Starting from KPIs and market and competitive data, we clarify the priority of initiatives and the rationale behind pricing, creating a state where decisions can be discussed in numbers rather than intuition.

A Principal's Perspective Cultivated Through Our Own Investments

As a company that runs its own businesses, B supply considers how to allocate a limited budget from the same perspective as our own investment decisions.

Flexible Involvement Suited to the Business Stage

From customer acquisition design at the seed stage to new product launches at TSE Prime-listed companies, we adjust the granularity of strategy and the scope of support to match the business's growth stage.
