SERVICEM&A STRATEGY

M&A Strategy

The more M&A deals a company handles, the less time it has to devote to strategic hypotheses and target screening.

M&A decision-making carries an inherent contradiction: the more deals there are, the less time is available for "rebuilding the strategic hypothesis" and "screening candidate companies." From our standpoint as investors who have made these decisions ourselves, we support the rebuilding of M&A strategy and business due diligence. We verify the market potential, revenue structure, and synergies of candidate companies and codify them as investment criteria usable at management meetings. Beyond strategy formulation, we provide end-to-end support through to building a sourcing foundation.

01SOLUTIONS ── Solutions

M&A Strategy

We redefine the objectives and criteria for acquisitions and build a strategic hypothesis viewed from the perspective of the overall business portfolio.

Sourcing Strategy & Foundation Construction

We design the discovery of candidate companies as a continuous system, building a structure that lets the company generate its own pipeline.

Business Due Diligence

We verify market potential, revenue structure, and synergies, clarifying the grounds for the acquisition decision.

PMI

We design the post-acquisition integration process and support the realization of anticipated synergies.

Business Succession

We support the redesign of the management structure and business structure that accompanies succession, drawing on our own practical experience as a principal.

Joint Ventures & Alliances

We bring business opportunities that could not succeed alone to fruition through partnership design.

02APPROACH ── What Sets Our Support Apart

Design from a Management Principal's Perspective

From our own standpoint as investors and operators who have made these decisions ourselves, we design criteria usable for the investment decision itself, not just an external evaluation. We do not produce strategy documents that are theory alone.

End-to-End Engagement from Strategy Formulation to Execution

From building the strategic hypothesis and conducting business DD on candidate companies to building the sourcing foundation, we support processes that are often disconnected from each other in one continuous engagement.

Translation into Decision Criteria Usable at Management Meetings

We codify the results of verifying market potential, revenue structure, and synergies as investment criteria, directly connecting them to the speed of decision-making at management meetings.

Connection to Building a Continuous Pipeline

Rather than stopping at a one-off strategic recommendation, we go as far as building a system that lets the company continue identifying candidate companies on its own.
