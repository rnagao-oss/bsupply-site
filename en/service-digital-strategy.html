SERVICEDIGITAL STRATEGY & PLANNING

Digital: Strategy & Planning

As generative AI reshapes business structures, how should the next move be decided? We support the rebuilding of digital strategy, from research through execution.

The advance of digital technologies, generative AI foremost among them, is reshaping business opportunity and competitive structure at the same time. B supply provides end-to-end support, from structured research on market and technology trends through identifying entry opportunities for the client and launching new digital services. Rather than stopping at research, we place emphasis on translating it into an executable strategy and initial business plan. From our standpoint as principals who run our own businesses, we present realistic initiatives that go beyond desk-based analysis.

01SOLUTIONS ── Solutions

Generative AI and Digital Market Research

We structure and research the impact of generative AI and digital technologies on existing and new businesses. We organize use cases and clarify entry opportunities for the client.

Digital Strategy Formulation

Based on the insights gained from research, we determine priority areas and the ranking of initiatives, shaping the output into materials that form the foundation for company-wide digital strategy discussions.

New Digital Service Planning

For the launch of a new digital service, we support everything from business concept development through initial roadmap formulation to launch execution.

02APPROACH ── What Sets Our Support Apart

Design from a Principal's Standpoint

From our standpoint as principals who run our own businesses, we ensure market research and strategy do not remain merely theoretical, translating them into executable plans. We design with an awareness of revenue structure from the planning stage onward.

End-to-End Engagement from Strategy to Execution

Rather than stopping at market research and strategy formulation, we continue to support the launch of new services and marketing execution. We share responsibility for executing the strategy we formulate.

Resolution on Business Structure

Beyond organizing technology trends, we structure our analysis all the way to its impact on the business model and revenue structure, organizing it at a level of detail that can withstand scrutiny at management meetings.

A Bias for Small-Scale Validation and Forward Progress

Because new digital domains carry high uncertainty, we refine the plan by incorporating small-scale validation, such as initial customer acquisition and early sales discussions, at an early stage.
