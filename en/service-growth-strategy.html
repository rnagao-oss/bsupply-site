SERVICEGROWTH STRATEGY

Growth Strategy

A strategy is not meant to remain merely theoretical.

B supply stays engaged end-to-end, not just describing a growth scenario but through execution and verification. Because we ourselves are principals who run our own businesses, we present realistic solutions that go beyond desk-based analysis. We provide support that directly informs management decisions, from mid-term business plans to business restructuring and the design of investment discipline. We combine the initiatives needed according to the company's business stage and growing pains.

01SOLUTIONS ── Solutions

Growth Strategy

We assess a business's room for growth and its constraints, and organize the initiatives and priorities needed to realize the next stage of growth.

Business Strategy

We redraw a business's path to winning, starting from redefining its value proposition, revenue structure, and competitive advantage.

Mid-Term Business Plan

We formulate a data-driven growth scenario and translate it into a plan usable for management decision-making.

Business Restructuring & Turnaround

For a deteriorating revenue structure or cash flow challenges, we redesign the business model and support its execution.

Investment Discipline Design

We codify the criteria for what to invest in and how much, supporting disciplined management execution.

02APPROACH ── What Sets Our Support Apart

Going as Far as the Numbers

Beyond just drawing up a strategy, we support data-driven decision-making through KPI analysis and P&L management.

Thinking of Capital and Strategy as Two Wheels of the Same Cart

We handle strategy and cash flow together, without separating them, including support for executing fundraising and building governance.

Taking On the Corporate Planning Function Itself

From formulating investment discipline to serving as an outsourced corporate planning function, we temporarily supplement the capabilities a company lacks in-house.

A Principal's Perspective

From our standpoint as principals who run our own businesses, we draw up realistic strategies grounded in feasibility.
