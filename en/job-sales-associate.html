RECRUITMANUFACTURING

Sales Associate

Bring sixty years of craftsmanship into customers' everyday lives.

A leather atelier in Mukojima, Sumida, founded in 1966. In this role you convey, in your own words, the quality that has long served leading brands as an OEM partner.

It's not just about selling: as the contact point for repair and maintenance, you support the long relationship between customers and their products — the kind of service only a store this close to the craftsmen can offer.

01RESPONSIBILITIES ── What You'll Do

  • Serve customers, sell leather goods, and propose gifts
  • Build customer records and grow repeat customers
  • Store operations: inventory, displays, and social media
  • Take in repair and maintenance requests and coordinate with the craftsmen

02PERSON ── Who We're Looking For

  • Customer service or sales experience (any industry)
  • Someone with a genuine attachment to craftsmanship and leather goods

03HIGHLIGHTS ── Why This Role

Be the point where brand meets customer

More than selling products, this role delivers the brand's value and the story behind the craft. Through conversation you convey each product's appeal and the joy of using it for years.

Turn customer voices into the business

Reactions gathered through daily service become key hints for product development, sales methods, and marketing. The voices you hear firsthand help make the brand and the business better.

Build sales fundamentals in practice

Customer understanding, proposals, relationship building, promotion — you hone the essentials of selling through real practice, growing by facing customers sincerely even in areas new to you.

Work close to the making

Working near production and product development, you understand how products are born and with what intent they are delivered — standing between makers and customers and connecting the two.

Help grow the brand itself

This is not a finished big-name brand but a business still being honed. Through sales, customer care, and improvement ideas, you contribute directly to its growth.
