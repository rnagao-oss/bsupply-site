Organization & Talent Strategy

The less a company can rely on brand recognition or scale, the more its recruiting requires genuine "strategy."

Organization and talent strategy is support that connects the "people" and "structure" that drive a business to its business strategy. B supply stays engaged end-to-end, from redefining the recruiting target through candidate sourcing, selection operations, and designing the organization's execution structure. We build talent requirements and organizational design based on the business's current reality, not on mission statements or slogans. As principals who run our own businesses, we design with an understanding of why recruiting and organization building stumble at the execution stage.

01SOLUTIONS ── Solutions

Recruiting Strategy

We redefine recruiting objectives in line with business strategy, and organize the target candidate profile and the priority of initiatives.

Talent Requirement Definition

We articulate the roles, skills, and orientations required at each business stage, translating them into criteria that function effectively in selection.

Candidate Sourcing & Selection Operations

We design outreach that generates applications from the target talent pool and build the operational structure for the selection process.

Organization & Execution Structure Design

We design the organizational structure and division of roles needed for hired talent to function effectively, aligning it with business operations.

02APPROACH ── What Sets Our Support Apart

Design from a Principal's Perspective

From our standpoint as principals who run our own businesses, we design recruiting not as a "system" but as a "management challenge." Rather than leading with mission statements, we define requirements grounded in the lived reality of business operations.

End-to-End Support from Strategy Formulation to Operational Execution

From redefining the recruiting target through candidate sourcing and selection operations, we stay engaged from start to finish so that initiatives never become disconnected.

Design Premised on Connection to Organizational Structure

Rather than separating recruiting and talent requirements from organizational and execution-structure design, we design people and organization together, tied directly to business strategy.
