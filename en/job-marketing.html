RECRUITMANUFACTURING

Marketing

Invent new ways to deliver sixty years of craft.

The craft and the quality are there. What's needed next is the power to bring them to the world. This role owns marketing for the leather business end to end — from launching our own brand and e-commerce to generating leads for new OEM clients.

We actively use technology, including AI, to deliver big results with a small team — working hand in hand with B supply's consulting team.

01RESPONSIBILITIES ── What You'll Do

Brand & E-commerce

Launch and run our own brand and e-commerce.

Content

Plan and execute social media and content marketing.

Lead Generation

Generate leads for new OEM clients through trade shows and the web.

Site Improvement

Plan and improve the brand site and product pages.

02REQUIREMENTS ── Qualifications

MUST

  • Hands-on experience in e-commerce or digital marketing

NICE TO HAVE

  • Experience launching a brand
  • Skills in content production such as photography and video

03PERSON ── Who We're Looking For

  • Someone who can run alongside the people making the products
  • Someone equally at home with numbers and creative work

04CONDITIONS ── Employment Terms

Employment Type

Full-time

Location

A combination of the Mukojima atelier, Tokyo stores, and remote work

Compensation

Determined under our internal policy based on experience, skills, and expected role. Detailed terms are presented individually through interviews and the selection process.
