SERVICENEW BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT

New Business Development & Execution

There is a great distance between a business idea and the ability to see it through to execution.

A new business gains value not at the moment it is conceived, but at the moment it enters the market and begins generating revenue. B supply does more than draw up a strategy: we assess the business's viability through a feasibility study and stay engaged through launch execution and initial customer acquisition and sales. We ourselves are principals who run our own businesses, and we are exacting about executable plans rather than theoretical ones. We handle everything from repurposing existing business assets for a new venture to building a business from scratch.

01SOLUTIONS ── Solutions

New Business Strategy Formulation

We weigh market opportunity against the company's own assets and strengths to build a business hypothesis with a genuine path to winning. In some cases, we begin by auditing existing assets.

Feasibility Study

We refine a business idea into a plan sufficient for an investment decision. We verify revenue structure and feasibility, and clarify whether to proceed.

Business Launch Execution

Rather than letting the strategy remain merely theoretical, we take responsibility for execution through to service launch. We stay engaged through the zero-to-one launch.

Initial Customer Acquisition & Sales Support

We support building the initial customer acquisition system and executing marketing, both essential for a business immediately after launch.

02APPROACH ── What Sets Our Support Apart

End-to-End Support from Strategy to Execution

Rather than stopping at strategy formulation, we take responsibility for staying engaged through launch execution and initial customer acquisition, avoiding the disconnect that occurs when the support provider changes at each phase.

A Genuine Stake as Principals Running Our Own Businesses

We ourselves are both investors and operators. In 2026, we succeeded a leather goods workshop in Mukojima, Tokyo, and are running its on-the-ground management.

The Ability to Conceive New Ideas Starting from Existing Assets

We take a perspective that audits assets a company already possesses, such as brand, customer base, and expertise, and uses them as the seeds of a new business.

Refining Plans That Can Withstand Investment Scrutiny

Through a feasibility study, we turn a mere idea into a concrete plan capable of convincing decision-makers.
