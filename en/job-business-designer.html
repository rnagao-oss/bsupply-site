RECRUITCONSULTING

Business Designer

Design the strategy — and see it through to results.

Consulting at B supply is not about delivering a report and moving on. From TSE Prime-listed companies to seed-stage startups and century-old businesses, our job is to move management decisions and execution forward.

As a business designer, you build hypotheses on clients’ management issues, analyze them, debate directly with executives, and stay alongside execution until results materialize. The role is fully remote, in an environment built for focusing on work that matters.

01RESPONSIBILITIES ── What You'll Do

Strategy & Execution

Drive strategy-development and execution-support projects for client companies.

Analysis & Infrastructure

Conduct market and competitive analysis, and build business plans and management-accounting foundations.

M&A & New Business

Support M&A strategy, business due diligence, and new business development.

Working with Executives

Build and test hypotheses through direct discussion with executives.

Group Businesses

Help design and execute value-up initiatives for group companies such as our leather manufacturing business.

02REQUIREMENTS ── Qualifications

MUST

  • Around two or more years in consulting, or in planning / business development at an operating company
  • Structured thinking and the drive to execute hands-on to the finish

NICE TO HAVE

  • Experience proposing to and driving projects with executives
  • Experience building management accounting or business plans
  • Involvement in M&A or new business development

03PERSON ── Who We're Looking For

  • Someone with the ownership to engage with executives in earnest
  • Someone who cares more about the business moving than about polished slides
  • Someone who keeps learning on their own in a fast-changing environment
