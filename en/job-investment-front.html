RECRUITPRINCIPAL INVESTMENT

Investment Front Member

See one company through — from the first meeting to its full potential.

B supply's principal investment business is different from funds that manage outside investors' money: we acquire small and medium-sized companies with our own capital and grow them over a long time horizon. We take over companies facing challenges such as the lack of a successor, then raise their value by bringing in the management expertise built through our consulting business and technologies such as AI.

As an investment front member, you will own the entire investment process — sourcing, analysis, negotiation, contracting, and post-acquisition management. There is no division of labor as in large organizations. Precisely because we are a small team, this is a position where you engage deeply in the decisions of a single company and feel, on the front line, how a business changes.

01RESPONSIBILITIES ── What You'll Do

Sourcing

Screen deal flow from M&A intermediaries and financial institutions, develop proprietary channels, and run initial screening against our investment criteria.

Due Diligence

Verify risks and growth hypotheses through business and financial analysis and interviews with owners and frontline teams.

Valuation & Negotiation

Drive valuation, deal structuring, and negotiations through to contract and closing.

PMI

Lead the post-acquisition transition: organization setup and management infrastructure such as accounting and KPIs.

Value-up

Design and execute improvement and growth initiatives together with the management team — including hands-on work on site.

Evolving the Playbook

Turn the lessons of each deal into sharper investment criteria and repeatable playbooks.

02WHY B SUPPLY ── Why This Role

Own the entire process

From sourcing to value-up, you work end to end rather than in silos — building the full skill set of an investor.

A long view, enabled by our own capital

Unlike funds with fixed terms, we are not built around a forced exit. You can keep making the choices that are best for the business and its people.

The shortest path to management

After succession, you help run the company as part of its management team. Real general-management experience is within reach, regardless of seniority.

03REQUIREMENTS ── Qualifications

MUST

  • At least around three years of professional experience
  • Hands-on experience in M&A, investment, finance, consulting, or corporate planning at an operating company
  • Ability to read the three financial statements and grasp a business's structure through the numbers

NICE TO HAVE

  • Hands-on M&A execution experience (due diligence, contracts, closing)
  • Experience in SME improvement, PMI, or turnaround
  • Experience launching a business or leading one as its head

04PERSON ── Who We're Looking For

  • Someone who wants to face the social issue of SME succession as a principal, not a bystander
  • Someone who doesn't stop at analysis and is willing to do unglamorous, hands-on work in the field
  • Someone who can build trust with people in very different positions — owners, employees, intermediaries
  • Someone who can form their own hypotheses and keep moving forward amid uncertainty

05CONDITIONS ── Employment Terms
